Slate privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Slate works entirely on your phone. It has no internet access, so nothing you do in Slate can be sent to us or to anyone else.

What Slate reads

Slate asks for permission to read and receive SMS. It uses this only to find transaction messages from banks and card issuers, such as debits, credits and card payments, so it can show your spending and balances. It reads messages from named senders only, and ignores one-time passwords and personal messages.

What Slate stores

  • The transactions it finds, the categories and notes you add, your monthly limit, balances, fixed payments and lent-and-borrowed entries.
  • All of this stays in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not backed up to any cloud by Slate.
  • Files you choose to export (the monthly spreadsheet file and the month-wrap picture) are saved to your phone's Downloads or Pictures folder, where they are under your control.

What Slate does not do

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No collection, sale or sharing of personal data. We never receive your data.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • No internet permission: the app cannot connect to any server.

Notifications

If you turn them on, Slate shows a notification on your phone after a spend, with what is left for today and buttons to sort the spend. These notifications are created on the phone and are not sent anywhere.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Start the month over to clear the current month, or uninstall Slate to delete everything it stored. Exported files stay in your Downloads or Pictures folder until you delete them.

Children

Slate is meant for adults managing their own money and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
